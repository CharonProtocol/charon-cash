03 — 07
The approach

Heavy math, light footprint.

Proofs are heavy math. Charon keeps them light — built on your device, small on the chain.

01

Deposit

Funds become an encrypted note only you can read — the chain sees a commitment.

02

Prove

Your device builds a zero-knowledge proof of ownership — without revealing the note.

03

Vanish

The chain verifies and settles. No sender, no receiver, no amount.

Built for Solana
  • Proofs on-device. The chain only verifies.
  • Any amount. Two-in, two-out — no fixed denominations.
  • Cheap by design. Fits Solana's compute budget.
  • Keyless relay. Broadcasts for you, holds nothing.
