01   Problem

Every Solana payment
is a public record.

Who paid whom. How much. A salary, an investment and even a gift payment can be reconstructed by anyone with a block explorer.

Who

Sender and receiver, linked to a public address forever.

How much

Every amount, readable by anyone with a browser.

When

A permanent, searchable history of your money.

02   Solution

A private balance
on a public chain.

01

Deposit / Send

Funds enter the shielded pool.

02

Shielded Transfer / Swap

Move and swap with nothing visible on-chain.

03

Payroll

Pay a whole team from one balance.

04

Withdraw

Exit to any address when you choose.

03   Product

One protocol.
Three ways in.

Charon Cash BETA

Send & swap

One shielded balance in an app. Private transfers and swaps. Amounts and recipients never show on-chain.

Payroll BETA

Pay a team

Upload a CSV, sign once. Every salary lands as a private note. Employees get their own app to see it.

Protocol & SDK

Build on it

On-chain program, in-browser prover and a relayer that holds no keys. Any app plugs in.
